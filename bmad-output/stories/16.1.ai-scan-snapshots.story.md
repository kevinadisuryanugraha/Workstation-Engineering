# 16.1: AI Scan Snapshots — Persist Live & Demo, History

**Story ID:** 16.1 · **Epic:** 16 · **Slug:** ai-scan-snapshots
status: done

## Story

As a **Tech Lead**, I want **every AI scan (live or demo) persisted as a snapshot with its findings**, so that **AI results become traceable evidence instead of ephemeral JSON**.

## Acceptance Criteria

<!-- LOCKED -->
1. Tabel `ai_scans` (id, projectId nullable, mode LIVE_ANALYSIS|STATIC_DEMO_PREVIEW, model, findings JSONB, recommendations JSONB, scannedBy, scanRef unique, createdAt) via migrasi.
2. `POST /api/ai/scan` (endpoint existing) kini MENYIMPAN hasil (live & demo) sebagai snapshot sebelum merespons; response menyertakan `scanRef`.
3. `GET /api/v1/ai/scans?projectId=&mode=&limit=` (PERM_AI_SCAN_TRIGGER) riwayat snapshot terbaru + filter.
4. `GET /api/v1/ai/scans/:scanRef` detail utuh snapshot; tidak ada → 404.
5. Snapshot immutable — tidak ada update/delete.
6. Jalur live Gemini tetap berfungsi bila GEMINI_API_KEY ada; tanpa key → tetap STATIC_DEMO_PREVIEW (perilaku SEC-05 tidak berubah).
