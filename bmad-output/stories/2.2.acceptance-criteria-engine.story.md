# 2.2: Acceptance Criteria Checklist Engine & Definition of Done Gate

**Story ID:** 2.2  
**Epic:** 2 — Work Items & Task Management  
**Slug:** acceptance-criteria-engine  
status: ready-for-dev  

> Owned by planning until `ready-for-dev`. Sized to one dev-day (~2-8h). No story points.

## Story

As a **Tech Lead / Developer**,  
I want **work items to have a structured checklist of acceptance criteria that blocks transitioning tasks to `DONE` unless 100% criteria are verified or an authorized override reason is supplied**,  
so that **"Done" represents real, verifiable progress rather than premature manual claims (enforcing the "Evidence over assumption" product principle)**.

## Acceptance Criteria

<!-- LOCKED. Numbered, testable. External dev tools must not edit. -->

1. Skema Drizzle `acceptance_criteria` dibuat di PostgreSQL dengan field: `id`, `work_item_id` (FK ke `work_items`), `text` (string deskripsi kriteria), `is_completed` (boolean default false), `completed_by` (FK ke `users`), `completed_at` (timestamp), `created_at`.
2. Endpoint `POST /api/v1/work-items/:id/acceptance-criteria` mengizinkan penambahan kriteria penerimaan ke suatu work item.
3. Endpoint `PATCH /api/v1/work-items/:id/acceptance-criteria/:criterionId` mengizinkan toggle status `is_completed` beserta pencatatan user yang mencentang dan timestamp-nya.
4. Saat status work item diubah ke `DONE` melalui `PATCH /api/v1/work-items/:id/status`:
   - Jika ada kriteria penerimaan yang bernilai `is_completed: false`, transisi DITOLAK dengan `HTTP 400 Bad Request` ("Cannot mark work item as DONE: 2 of 5 acceptance criteria are incomplete").
   - Pengecualian (*override*): Hanya Tech Lead, PM, atau Admin yang dapat meloloskan status `DONE` dengan kriteria belum lengkap, WAJIB menyertakan field `overrideReason` (minimal 10 karakter) yang dicatat ke audit log.

## Testing

<!-- LOCKED. STRATEGY ONLY — what to verify and how. -->

- **Approach:** Integration testing state transition.
- **Key scenarios:**
  1. Work item dengan 3 kriteria (1 belum centang) gagal dipindahkan ke DONE (HTTP 400).
  2. Begitu kriteria ke-3 dicentang, transisi ke DONE sukses (HTTP 200).
  3. Developer biasa gagal melakukan override saat kriteria belum lengkap (HTTP 403).
  4. Tech Lead berhasil melakukan override dengan menyertakan `overrideReason: "Hotfix deployed to production with deferred docs"`.
