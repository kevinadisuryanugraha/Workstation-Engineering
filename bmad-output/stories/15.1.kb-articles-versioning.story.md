# 15.1: KB Articles, Versioning & Draft from Resolved Ticket

**Story ID:** 15.1 · **Epic:** 15 · **Slug:** kb-articles-versioning
status: done

## Story

As a **Support / Developer**, I want **knowledge articles with version history, including one-click drafts from resolved tickets**, so that **solutions are captured once and reused forever**.

## Acceptance Criteria

<!-- LOCKED -->
1. Tabel `kb_articles` (slug unique, title, body TEXT markdown, tags JSONB array, version, ownerName, sourceTicketKey nullable, projectId nullable) dan `kb_article_versions` (articleId FK, version, title, body, updatedAt).
2. CRUD: `POST/GET/PATCH /api/v1/kb` (lihat: PERM_VIEW_DASHBOARD; buat/ubah: PERM_WORK_ITEM_UPDATE). Setiap PATCH membuat baris versi baru; versi lama tidak pernah diubah/dihapus.
3. `GET /api/v1/kb/:slug/versions` → daftar riwayat versi; `GET /api/v1/kb/:slug/versions/:version` → isi versi spesifik.
4. `POST /api/v1/kb/from-ticket` body `{key}`: tiket harus RESOLVED/CLOSED (selain itu 400); membuat draft artikel (slug auto dari key, body prefilled dari judul + deskripsi tiket, sourceTicketKey terisi).
5. Filter list `?tag=&q=` pada judul.
